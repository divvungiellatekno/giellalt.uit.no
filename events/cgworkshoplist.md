
Nordic workshops in constraint grammar
==============

There have been CG (from 2025: CG and FST) workshops at each NoDaLiDa
since 2007, with the exception of the Covid year 2021, where NoDaLiDa
was arranged as an online conference.

1. **2007** [CG workshop at the NoDaLiDa 2007 conference in Tarto](https://giellatekno.uit.no/cg/ee07/index.html)
2. **2009** [CG workshop at the NoDaLiDa 2009 conference in Odense](https://giellatekno.uit.no/cg/09/index.html)
3. **2011** [CG workshop at the NoDaLiDa 2011 conference in Riga](https://giellatekno.uit.no/cg/11/index.html)
4. **2013** [CG workshop at the NoDaLiDa 2013 conference in Oslo](https://giellatekno.uit.no/cg/13/index.html)
5. **2015** [CG workshop at the NoDaLiDa 2015 conference in Vilnius](https://ep.liu.se/en/conference-issue.aspx?series=ecp&issue=113)
6. **2017** [CG workshop at the NoDaLiDa 1017 conference in Gothenburg](https://edu.visl.dk/nodalida2017.html)
7. **2019** [CG workshop at the NoDaLiDa 1019 conference in Turku](https://edu.visl.dk/nodalida2019.html)
8. **2023** [CG workshop at the NoDaLiDa 1023 conference in Torshavn](https://edu.visl.dk/nodalida2023.html)
9. **2025** [CG and FST workshop at the NoDaLiDa 1025 conference in Tallinn](https://divvungiellatekno.github.io/giellalt.uit.no/events/2025-cg/)
